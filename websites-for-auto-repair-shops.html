Auto repair shops

Websites for independent auto repair shops

I spent close to two decades in the automotive world, more than 15 of them as a technician, so I know how customers pick a shop and what they need from your website: what you fix, when you're open, how to reach you, and why they can trust you.

What your site needs

  • Your services, in plain words: repair, diagnostics, hybrid and electric work, whatever you do.
  • Hours, map and click-to-call: so a driver with a check engine light can reach you in one tap.
  • Trust builders: certifications, reviews and photos of your real shop.
  • Fast on phones: most people searching for a shop are on their phone, often on the road.
  • Easy updates: change hours or specials yourself, with training included.

Real example

I built the website for Liberti's Auto Electric, an independent repair shop, in close collaboration with the owner: repair and hybrid services, ASE certification, an FAQ, reviews, hours, a map and click-to-call links.

What it costs

Shop websites usually fall in the small business range, starting at $1,200. Tell me about your shop and I'll give you a realistic estimate.
