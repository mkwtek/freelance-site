Nonprofits

Websites for nonprofits

At a nonprofit-focused web agency, I built and supported websites for more than a dozen nonprofits, from salmon recovery to open adoption to mission-driven staffing. I know what small teams are juggling: tight budgets, many hands updating the site, and a mission that has to come through clearly.

What you get

  • Accessible by design: tested to WCAG 2.2 AA by hand and with tools, with no overlay widgets.
  • Easy for staff to update: a lean CMS setup and live training over virtual video meetings.
  • Clear calls to action: donate, volunteer, sign up or get help, easy to find on any device.
  • Yours to keep: your domain and accounts stay in your organization's name.

Deadlines to know

Organizations funded by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services must meet WCAG 2.1 AA by May 2027, or May 2028 with fewer than 15 employees. An audit now leaves time to fix problems on a schedule instead of in a rush.

What it costs

Accessibility audits start at $450, and small sites start at $1,200. Tell me about your organization and I'll give you a realistic estimate.
