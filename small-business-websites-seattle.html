Small businesses

Small business websites in Seattle

A website should bring in customers, not just sit there looking nice. I build fast, accessible sites for small businesses, test them on real phones, and explain everything in plain English so you stay in charge of your own site. I'm based in the Seattle area and work remotely with clients anywhere in the U.S. and Canada.

What you get

  • A site planned around your customers: what they need to find, and the call, booking or visit you want from them.
  • Fast on phones: sized images, lean code and testing on real devices.
  • Found on Google the honest way: organic SEO: clear structure, accurate titles and descriptions, and a sitemap.
  • Accessible: tested to WCAG 2.2 AA by hand and with tools.
  • Yours to keep: your domain and accounts stay in your name.

What it costs

Small business sites (up to 5 pages) start at $1,200, and most launch in about 3 to 6 weeks. Seattle-area freelancers typically charge $2,500 to $7,500, and agencies start around $5,000. Added features, like online booking or a store, raise the price. Your price depends on the scope we agree on.
